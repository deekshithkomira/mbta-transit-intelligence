## Short-Hop distance-based fare

- Today Copley → Arlington (1 stop) and Copley → Medford/Tufts (13 stops, 6.0 track miles) both cost $2.40.
- Short-Hop rule: riding a line end to end costs $2.40; shorter trips pay in proportion to distance (Washington-style composite miles, rounded up to $0.05), minimum $0.80.
- Copley → Medford/Tufts = 5.24 of 7.05 composite miles on the whole Green Line E = $1.80; Copley → Arlington = the $0.80 minimum. Every end-to-end trip (Heath Street → Medford/Tufts, Alewife → Braintree, Oak Grove → Forest Hills, Wonderland → Bowdoin) costs exactly $2.40.
- Reduced fares (students, seniors) are half price; a missing tap-out is charged $2.40.
